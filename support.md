# Puffin' Support

_Last updated: 20 August 2026_

Puffin' turns the workouts you're already doing into group challenges with your
friends. If something isn't working, or you've seen something you shouldn't have,
this page is how you reach us.

## Contact us

**Luke@blueskylabs.io**

We answer every message, and we aim to reply within 24 hours. If you're reporting
abusive content or another person's behaviour, say so in the subject line and we
will prioritise it.

You can also reach this page from inside the app: **Settings → Report a problem**.

## Reporting content or blocking someone

You don't have to email us to deal with someone. In the app:

- **Report a workout or comment** — press and hold it, or swipe, and choose Report.
  Tell us what's wrong with it and we'll review it.
- **Block a person** — open their profile and choose Block. Blocking is total and
  goes both ways: neither of you will see the other's workouts, comments, reactions
  or profile in any feed, and neither of you appears in the other's standings.
- **Leave a group** — open the group, then Leave. Your past workouts stop appearing
  in that group's feed.

We review every report and remove content that breaks our
[Terms of Use](/terms-of-use/).

## Common questions

### My workouts aren't showing up

Almost always this is Health permissions. iOS doesn't tell an app when its read
access has been refused, so a blocked permission looks exactly like having no
workouts at all.

Open **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Puffin'** and make sure every
category is on. Then in Puffin', go to **Settings → Re-import everything from
Health**. That's always safe to run — it can't create duplicates.

If the feed still looks empty, check you've actually joined a group and that
someone in it has recorded something.

### How do I join a group?

Ask whoever made it for the six-character invite code, then **Groups → Join** and
type it in. To start your own, use **Groups → New group** and share the code it
gives you.

### What counts toward a challenge?

Any workout you record that starts inside the challenge's window. A run that begins
before the challenge ends and finishes afterwards still counts in full.

A workout is never used up by one challenge. Ten miles counts, in full, toward a
five-mile challenge in one group *and* a ten-mile challenge in another — nothing is
split between them. The chips underneath each workout in the feed show you exactly
what it counted toward.

### Why does my steps challenge have standings but no workout list?

Steps come from your daily totals in Apple Health rather than from individual
workouts, so there's no workout to attach them to. The standings are still correct.

### Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Puffin' reads whatever is already in Apple Health, from any watch, phone or
app. If you don't have a watch, the **Activity** tab can record a workout itself —
with a map of your route for outdoor sessions, and a live view on the Lock Screen
and in the Dynamic Island. You can also log a workout by hand if you did it
without your phone.

### Where do my routes go?

Nowhere. GPS routes are saved to Apple Health on your iPhone and are drawn from
there. They are never uploaded to our servers.

### Who can see my workouts?

Only people in the groups you've joined. There is no public feed, no follower
count, and no way to be discovered by strangers. The full details are in our
[Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).

### How do I delete my account?

**Settings → Delete account.** This permanently removes your profile, your
workouts, your comments and reactions, and your group memberships, and disconnects
Puffin' from your Apple ID. It cannot be undone.

Deleting the app on its own doesn't delete your account — use the button first.

### Puffin' isn't reading my workouts automatically any more

iOS throttles background updates when Low Power Mode is on, and stops them
entirely if you force-quit an app. Opening Puffin' always triggers a full sync, so
the feed catches up the moment you come back to it.

## Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Sign in with Apple is required. Puffin' needs
permission to read workouts, steps and active energy from Apple Health.
