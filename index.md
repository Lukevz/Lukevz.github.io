# Puffin'

Group fitness challenges, built on Apple Health. Make a group, share the
six-character code, set a challenge — and every workout you record counts toward
it automatically.

- [Support](/support/) — contact us, and answers to common questions

Puffin' is available on iPhone, running iOS 18 or later.
